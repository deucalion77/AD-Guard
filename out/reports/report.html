Dangerous delegated rights detected on high-value AD container

Evidence

  "object": "domain_controllers_ou",
  "principal": "CORP\\john.smith",
  "principal_sam": "john.smith",
  "rights": [
    "CreateChild",
    "DeleteChild",
    "ExtendedRight",
    "GenericAll",
    "Self",
    "WriteDacl",
    "WriteOwner",
    "WriteProperty"
  ],
  "activeDirectoryRights_raw": 983551,
  "accessControlType_raw": 0,
  "source_file": "domain_controllers_ou.json"
}
Impact:
Explicit powerful ACLs on Tier-0 containers can enable privilege escalation or domain takeover depending on the principal and target objects. See Microsoft's Privileged Access documentation: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security/privileged-access-workstations/
Remediation:
  • Remove unnecessary delegated rights (GenericAll/GenericWrite/WriteDACL/WriteOwner/Extended rights).
  • Restrict Tier-0 ACLs (Domain Controllers OU, privileged groups) to tightly controlled admin groups.
  • Re-run scan to confirm exposure is removed.

  "ms-DS-MachineAccountQuota": 10
}
Impact:
Allows authenticated users to join machines to the domain, which can enable certain abuse paths in misconfigured environments.
